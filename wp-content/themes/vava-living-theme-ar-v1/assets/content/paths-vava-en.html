Session FAQ

Before booking guidance

These questions are for anyone who is closer to choosing a session or package and wants to understand the options, expectations, and boundaries of the experience.

How do I choose what suits me?

If you need initial understanding and a clear plan, the Balance Map is a gentle starting point. If you want application and follow-up, the Balance Journey may be closer. Deep Healing is for those who need deeper work and more continuity.

Do I need one session or a package?

One session gives understanding and an initial plan, while deeper balance often needs follow-up and adjustment. Packages are usually better for anyone seeking application and continuity.

When might I notice results?

This differs from person to person depending on the situation and level of commitment. Some shifts may appear within days, while deeper balance may need weeks of continuity.

Are the recommendations difficult to apply?

No. The aim is to simplify life, not complicate it. Recommendations are shaped around lifestyle, Ayurvedic constitution, and realistic capacity for commitment.

Do the sessions replace medical care?

No. The sessions support general balance and lifestyle. They are not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment, and no medications or medical treatments are provided.

Can I ask questions after the session?

Yes. Questions can be asked during the support period specified for each package.

I still do not know which package suits me. What should I do?

You can send a message through the contact page to clarify the most suitable starting point without pressure.

Is there a questionnaire before the session?

Yes. When booking any session except follow-up sessions, a questionnaire is sent to help make the experience more precise and suitable.

In closing

There is no single way to enter VAVA. Some begin through a session, some through an idea, some through a product, and some through a quiet moment of recognition.
